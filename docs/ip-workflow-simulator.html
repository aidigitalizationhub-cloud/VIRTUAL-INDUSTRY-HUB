Submission policy and terms

Submitting a disclosure is the first formal step for appropriate institutional review. Researchers should submit innovative work with potential economic value as soon as possible and before public disclosure. Premature public disclosure may affect protection and commercialization options.

Ownership and agreements

Ownership can depend on University resources, employment or study, sponsorship, government funding, collaboration, confidentiality agreements, material-transfer agreements, and other obligations. The researcher must disclose relevant background IP, contributors, funding, and agreements. The Research and Innovation Directorate/TTO evaluates the disclosure and may consult experts.

AI and publication

AI findings are advisory. The researcher understands that a human Admin, TTO/IP officer, or authorized Super Admin makes the platform decision. Only approved public information is published; restricted and confidential files are not public.
